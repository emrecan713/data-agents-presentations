## Responding like someone without being any one thing

<div class="rd">
<div>
<p class="black">“It adapts to what you want it to be, <em>but it is not really any one thing.</em>”</p>
<p class="src">A qualitative researcher, workshop roundtable</p>
<div class="qs">
<div><h3>An ontological question</h3><p>“What is AI?”</p></div>
<div><h3>A relational question</h3><p>“What is AI to me?”</p></div>
</div>
</div>
<img src="assets/person-laptop.svg" alt="Line drawing of a person at a desk talking with a laptop; a speech bubble rises from each of them.">
</div>

<p class="def"><strong>Relational dissonance:</strong> the divergence between how people frame AI and the relationships that develop as they interact with it. The tension is part of the structure of the interface, so changing our beliefs cannot remove it.</p>

## What we can and cannot control

<p class="banner">Individual strategies can only have limited influence over these systems. The interface pulls us into a back and forth conversation whose trajectory neither the researcher nor the AI fully controls.</p>

<div class="honest">
<div class="head">Worth doing</div>
<div class="do">Check what the system received, and check its quotes against the transcript.</div>
<div class="do">Read the exchange alongside the interview, and note why you used a particular formulation.</div>
<div class="do">Go beyond saying that “AI helped”: explain how the interpretation developed.</div>
</div>
